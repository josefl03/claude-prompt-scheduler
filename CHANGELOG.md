## 0.1.0

- First public release, extracted from a personal plugin.
- Prompt prefixes `@17:00`, `@tomorrow 9:00`, `@2h30m`, `@done`, `@reset`; commands `/schedule-at`, `/schedule-menu`, `/schedule-queue`, `/schedule-cancel`, `/schedule-resume`.
- The queue, the band, the footer and the panel show only the current session's prompts; `/schedule-queue all` lists every session's.
- Exact firing with one timer per prompt; usage-limit resume kept as a backup of Claude Code's own.
